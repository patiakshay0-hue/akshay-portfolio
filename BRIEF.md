# BRIEF: Akshay Patil portfolio

> Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. The owner supplied a complete
> written brief and said to make design decisions independently, so the eight
> ScrollCraft interview topics below are authored decisions, not interview answers.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Graphite control room. Warm white type, one lime accent, fine grid.
   References (non-web): a lab bench oscilloscope, a flight-strip board, a
   printed circuit schematic.
2. **Journey (owner's order).** Discover what I build, watch a system come alive,
   explore projects, understand the workflow, see the dev range, learn the
   approach, make contact.
3. **Energy curve.** Calm confident open, rising through the pipeline climax,
   steady and informational through case study and projects, quiet at about,
   resolved and warm at contact.
4. **Feeling / peak.** See the feeling curve below. Peak: the pipeline connects
   and a Telegram message assembles.
5. **Never-seen-before move (signature).** *Scroll is the run.* In the Pipeline
   act the scroll position is the execution clock of one real record: every
   stage runs real classification and scoring code on it, and a stage trace
   keeps the marks the visitor has passed.
6. **Range.** Premium dark technical, with dense monospaced instrument detail.
7. **One world or distinct scenes.** Distinct scenes. No continuous flight.
8. **Assets.** One portrait photo supplied by the owner (About section). No
   video, no generated imagery. Everything else is HTML, CSS and SVG.

## Grammar

Act-based scenes on the ScrollCraft engine, with one **live surface** (the
Automation Lab) that is operable markup running real logic on clearly labelled
fictional sample data. The owner's brief requires a nav bar, a hero and a CTA
close, so this is not a pure Live-surface grammar. Filmic one-shot lost because
there is no footage and the page is a tool-led argument, not a single film.
Chaptered editorial, poster, gallery, split stage, continuous world and cutlist
lost because the page needs a working simulation, a project explorer and a
persuasive close in one document.

assembled itself, and the numbers in it were computed live."* Act: Pipeline.
